## 2. Executive Summary & Proposed Business Objectives

For a dynamic manufacturing and distribution operation, BizLens delivers immediate impact across six core strategic objectives:
1. **Establish a Single Source of Truth:** Eliminate fragmented spreadsheets and isolated records with a single, controlled operational and accounting database.
2. **Raw Material & WIP Traceability:** Full visibility across bulk raw materials, premixes, packaging, and finished goods across factory floors and remote depots.
3. **Accurate Batch Costing & Variance Analysis:** Capture direct labor, generator diesel, and factory overhead per batch; expose discrepancies between standard recipe and actual consumption.
4. **Distributor Credit & Receivables Control:** Automated 30/60/90-day Accounts Receivable aging to prevent working capital from being trapped in overdue debts.
5. **Multi-Tier Anti-Fraud Governance:** Enforce strict 4-eye payment approval (*Prepared $\rightarrow$ Approved $\rightarrow$ Authorized $\rightarrow$ Paid*) and 100% immutable audit logging.
6. **Instant Financial Reporting:** Generate real-time IFRS-compliant Balance Sheets, P&L statements, and Cash Flow statements at the click of a button.

* **C. Price Stability on Renewals:** Renewal fees are fixed at the agreed rate for the first two (2) years. Subsequent annual adjustments are capped at a maximum of 5%–10% to accommodate foreign-exchange fluctuations on cloud hosting, provided with at least 60 days advance written notice.

* **E. Definition of Perpetual Lifetime License:** Grants Value Ingredients Ltd a perpetual, non-exclusive right to run the licensed version indefinitely without recurring license fees. Includes 12 months of complimentary bug fixes and critical security patches.
* **F. Major Version Upgrades (Desktop/Intranet):** Minor updates (e.g., v1.1, v1.2) are provided free. Major architectural overhauls (e.g., v2.0 introducing brand-new modules) are optional and offered to perpetual license holders at a 50% discount.
* **G. Complimentary Support & Hypercare:** All deployments include ninety (90) days of complimentary hypercare and direct technical support from go-live sign-off.
* **H. Post-Warranty Maintenance & Support:** After 90 days, the client may subscribe to an optional Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) at 15% of the initial software fee per annum (covering priority support, quarterly system health checks, and patches) or opt for ad-hoc pay-per-incident support.
* **I. PC Replacement & Hardware Reinstallation:** Reinstallation and database restoration onto a replacement workstation due to hardware failure is complimentary within the first 12 months (up to 2 occurrences). Subsequent reinstalls without an active AMC attract a nominal engineering fee of ₦50,000.

## 6. Assumptions, Exclusions & Scope Boundaries

* **Historical Data Migration Scope:** Migration is strictly limited to Master Data (Chart of Accounts, Item Master with SKUs/UOMs, Customer and Vendor directories, Asset Register) and Opening Balances (Stock on Hand quantities/values, customer AR balances, vendor AP balances, and General Ledger opening trial balance) as of the agreed cut-over date. Granular historical multi-year line-by-line transaction reconstruction is excluded from the standard fee and can be separately scoped.
* **Customization & Bespoke Engineering:** The quoted investment covers full standard configuration of BizLens workflows, user roles, BOM recipes, and document templates. Bespoke functional development, custom API integrations, or non-standard custom reports outside core capabilities will be evaluated and priced under a Formal Change Request (FCR).
* **Hardware & Infrastructure Requirements:** Value Ingredients Ltd is responsible for providing suitable client hardware and infrastructure:
  * *Cloud Model:* Workstations/Laptops with Intel Core i3 or higher, 8GB RAM, Windows 10/11 or macOS, and a stable 5–10 Mbps internet connection.
  * *On-Premise / Intranet Model:* Dedicated Server PC (Intel Core i5/i7/Xeon, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, Gigabit Ethernet NIC, Windows 10/11 Pro or Linux LTS); Structured Cat6 Gigabit Local Area Network (LAN); Reliable Inverter / Online UPS (minimum 2-hour backup) to prevent database corruption from sudden power outages.
* **Statutory & Tax Configuration:** **IMPORTANT NOTE: There is currently no tax configuration in the BizLens system.** The software processes transaction gross values, discounts, and net amounts. Basic tax calculation fields (e.g. standard VAT toggle) can be introduced via configuration. Major statutory overhauls (e.g. live e-invoicing integrations with FIRS/NRS or state revenue boards) are excluded from this base scope and will be implemented collaboratively under future regulatory maintenance updates.
* **Data Ownership & Confidentiality:** Value Ingredients Ltd retains 100% exclusive, unencumbered ownership of all operational, financial, customer, vendor, and formulation data. CodySense claims zero rights over client data and executes a legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). All cloud-hosted data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
* **Software Intellectual Property:** CodySense IT Solutions retains all proprietary ownership, source code, copyright, and intellectual property rights in BizLens. Value Ingredients Ltd is granted a non-exclusive license to use the software for its legitimate business operations.
* **Acceptance Criteria & User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** Milestones are accepted via a signed UAT Sign-Off Certificate. During UAT, defects logged in the Issue Tracker are classified as Severity 1 (Critical - system unusable), Severity 2 (Major - core function impaired), or Severity 3 (Minor - aesthetic/non-blocking). Go-live sign-off requires resolution of all Severity 1 and 2 issues.
* **Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery:** For Cloud deployments, CodySense manages daily off-site cloud backups and automated recovery. For Desktop and Intranet on-premise deployments, CodySense will configure an automated local backup script to an external drive/network location, and train the client's internal IT officer on daily off-site copy routines.
* **Third-Party Integrations:** Direct API integrations with commercial bank portals (direct debit/NIBSS), external POS merchant gateways, third-party payroll engines, or external e-commerce webshops are excluded from the base scope and available via separate integration contracts.
